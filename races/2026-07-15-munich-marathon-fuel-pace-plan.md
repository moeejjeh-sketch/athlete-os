## Fueling plan

**Carbs: 90g/hour**, from ~15 minutes in through the finish. This is at the
practical ceiling identified in the research (lab-observed peak ~105g/hr,
90g/hr is the realistic sustained target) — **flagged**: stick to
gut-trained glucose:fructose products only, don't introduce anything new on
race day even though you've proven 90g/hr works in training.

- Total carb need over ~2:50: **~255g**
- Timing: **1 gel (~30g, 2:1 glucose:fructose) every 20 minutes**, starting
  at ~15min — that's 3/hour = 90g/hr
- The course hands out gels at 14km and 28km (per the dossier) — treat
  those as 2 of your ~8-9 total gels, not extras on top; carry the rest
  yourself since finisher reports specifically flagged the default
  14km/28km timing as insufficient on its own

**Fluid: ~0.9 L/hour (ESTIMATE)** — math: population sweat-rate average is
1.28±0.57 L/hr generally, but that spans all conditions; in Munich's cool
8-14°C range, the lower end of that band applies (~0.8-1.0 L/hr), so 0.9
L/hr is the point estimate used here.

- Total fluid need over ~2:50: **~2.5L**
- Timing: aid stations run every 2.5-3km (roughly every 10-13min at goal
  pace) — drink **~150-180ml at every aid station** to hit the target
  without over- or under-drinking between stops

**Sodium: ~900mg/hour (ESTIMATE, derived)** — math: 0.9 L/hr × 1,000mg/L
(estimated sweat sodium concentration) = 900mg/hr.

- Course isotonic drink's exact sodium content is unknown (**uncertain**,
  not in the dossier) — don't assume it fully covers this number
- Practical approach: 1 electrolyte capsule (~300-400mg sodium) roughly
  every 40-45 minutes, adjusted down if the course isotonic drink is used
  regularly and up if it isn't

## Pacing plan

Target A-goal average: **3:59/km**. Structured as even-to-slightly-negative
per the pacing research (non-elite marathoners consistently run faster
overall with this shape than with an even-hotter start) — this also
directly targets the exact failure mode flagged in the race dossier:
finishers repeatedly went out too fast on this flat, deceptively-easy-
feeling course and paid for it late.

| Segment | Target pace | Why |
|---|---|---|
| km 0-5 | 4:03-4:05/km | Deliberately conservative — the course "feels" easy here per multiple finisher reports; that feeling is not a reliable signal |
| km 5-30 | 3:58-4:00/km | Goal pace, steady, aid-station fueling rhythm locked in |
| km 30-37 | 3:58-4:00/km | Hold pace through the Marienplatz high and the quieter stretch after it |
| km 37-40 | **4:05-4:10/km (ESTIMATE)** | Course climbs here (~90-100m total course gain concentrates in this section per the dossier — exact grade isn't published, so this adjustment is estimated, not GAP-calculated precisely). Let effective effort/HR stay steady in Z4 rather than forcing pace on the climb |
| km 40-42.2 | Sub-4:00/km if legs allow | Flat run to the Olympiapark tunnel finish — empty the tank |

No heat adjustment applied (race temp sits below the 15°C threshold where
it would start to matter) — if race-day conditions run warmer than the
historical average, apply ~1-2 sec/km slower per °C above 15°C (roughly
double that if humid).

## What to do with the estimates before race day

1. **Sweat rate test**: weigh nude before/after a long run in similar cool
   conditions, account for fluid consumed — replaces the 0.9 L/hr estimate
   with your real number.
2. **Sweat sodium**: a patch test or lab analysis replaces the 1,000mg/L
   estimate — matters more for Hamburg (9+ hours) than here, but establishing
   it now means it's ready for both.
3. **Rehearse this exact plan** (90g/hr, this pacing shape) on at least one
   long run before race day — race day should never be the first time you
   run this plan, only the first time it counts.
