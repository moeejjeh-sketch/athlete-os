# My Athlete OS

This is the one-page overview of my personal endurance training system. It
will fill in over the next 7 days as I build it out.

## How it's organized
- **athlete-profile.md** — who I am: goals, races, constraints, zones
- **training/** — my training plans and weekly schedule
- **reviews/** — my weekly workout reviews and analysis
- **races/** — my race research and race-day plans
- **health/** — my recovery, readiness, and injury-risk notes
- **.claude/skills/** — reusable skills I can re-run with one command

## The idea
My training data lives in Strava (the source of truth, read live). These
files are my memory — profile, plans, reviews, and race plans — kept current
between sessions. Each day this week I'll add another piece until my Athlete
OS is complete.
